## Notes

**Note 1, Işıkçelik 2026 Table 3 column order.** Table 2 defines K2 as Yetkili Sağlık Tesisi (hospitals, medical centres, clinics and other facilities authorised by the Ministry), K3 as authorised intermediaries, K4 as licensed accommodation facilities and K5 as their beds. The Table 3 columns as extracted do not fit these definitions. The column headed K2 and the column headed K3 behave like facilities and beds: Antalya 2.637 and 614.252, Muğla 2.844 and 226.259, about 233 and 80 beds per facility. The Discussion says Antalya has the highest K5 (bed count), yet the column headed K5 peaks in İstanbul at 797. The likely data order is K1, K4, K5, K2, K3, which would make the column headed K4 (sum 5.935 over 41 provinces) the count of authorised health facilities of all types. This is an inference from the page and was not checked against the printed PDF layout. authorised_by_province.csv keeps hospitals and medical_centres as "not shown", because the paper gives no split by facility type, and carries both columns in the note.

**Note 2, "public" hospitals.** The brief describes the 16 physical medicine and rehabilitation hospitals as public. Table 7.1 has no sector column and its total row (1.562 hospitals, 268.359 beds) covers all rows, so the page does not show that these 16 are public. The row is recorded without that label.

**Note 3, names and labels.** Province names follow Table 10.12: Elâzığ and Hakkâri in S07 are written Elazığ and Hakkari, Afyon in S06 is written Afyonkarahisar, and the row printed as Çorlu in S06 is mapped to Tekirdağ. In radiotherapy.csv, "REGION:" rows carry the English region names printed in S05 and are not provinces.

**Note 4, yearbook items by province not extracted.** Listed in the 2024 index, outside the requested files: Harita 7.6 (haemodialysis devices per 1.000.000, by province), Şekil 7.15 (neonatal intensive care beds per 1.000 live births, by province), Tablo 7.20, 8.26 and 12.4 (provincial indicators), Tablo 13.5, 13.6, 13.12 and 13.13 (disability-related incidence by province).

**Note 5, which provinces can receive patients.** The Ministry of Health's four lists of authorised facilities (https://shgmturizmdb.saglik.gov.tr/TR-76664/yetkili-saglik-tesisleri.html) decline automated reading, so they were not opened. Provinces are therefore set in `src/build_model.py` from S23 (regional counts), S07, S24 and S25 (named provinces). In each region the named provinces come first and the remaining places go to the provinces with the most specialist physicians: 44 provinces are named and 16 are inferred. S07 excluded provinces with incomplete data, so a province outside it may still hold a certificate. A list taken from the Ministry's pages can replace this file set directly.

**Note 6, check on the estimate of hospitals by province.** Authorised hospitals in a region are shared among its provinces by the geometric mean of their hospitals and their authorised facilities. Before Antalya's own count was added, this gave Antalya 34 hospitals, against 32 in the Ministry lists of July 2026 (S26). Erzurum came out at 3.8, against three hospitals with the certificate in December 2018 (Çavuş, A., & Gönenbaba, İ. (2020). Erzurum'daki Hastanelerin Sağlık Turizmi Açısından Değerlendirilmesi. Atatürk Üniversitesi Sosyal Bilimler Enstitüsü Dergisi, 24(4), 1681-1693. https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/ataunisosbil/issue/59389/654143).

## Gaps

1. **Burn care.** No facility list by province was found and no intensive care bed figure. Only national totals (28 centres, 48 units, 840 beds, 2025) and one named facility.
2. **Rehabilitation.** The names and provinces of the 16 hospitals in Table 7.1 were not found, and no beds by hospital. Three FTR hospitals are named on opened pages and two more from search titles only. S08 gives 18 hospitals for 2023 and S03 gives 16 for 2024, without explanation. No spinal cord injury units, and no paediatric rehabilitation beyond one national table row. Amputee care: one search title only.
3. **Prosthetics and orthotics.** No count of centres, workshops or staff was read, nationally or by province. prosthetics.csv was not written.
4. **Radiotherapy.** No provincial breakdown. Regional counts and six-city densities only, dated December 2019. Paediatric oncology units: nothing found.
5. **Tertiary centres.** Heart centres by province are from 2009 and do not separate adult from congenital. Congenital centres are national only, 2009. Bone marrow transplant centres are national only, January 2014. Organ transplant centres: no count, one search title only.
6. **Specialists by branch.** No table by branch and province was found. Table 10.12 gives all-branch totals by province. Among the requested branches, only radiation oncology has a national count (596, December 2019).
7. **Authorised facilities.** 41 provinces only, no hospital and medical centre split, column order uncertain (Note 1). The Ministry figures of 654 hospitals and 189 medical centres (July 2024) were not read on an opened page and are not recorded.
8. **Health tourists by nationality and field, 2023 to 2025.** Nothing was found for these years, so ht_by_nationality.csv was not written. Read outside the window (S13): 388.150 health tourists in 2020 and 642.444 in 2021 (TÜİK, as cited by Cihat Alagöz), and about 400.000 from Europe in 2021 (his estimate). S14 lists treatment fields attributed to USHAŞ without numbers. No TÜİK or USHAŞ page was opened.
