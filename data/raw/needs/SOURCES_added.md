Rows for GOSNEY_2013, YILMAZ_2023, EC_COVID_2020, HANNA_2020, ANKAH_2025, DHA_2023 and YAGCI_2023 are the extraction made when the sources were first read. Full citations are in `../../../src/needs_table.py` and `../../../docs/needs_table.md`.
