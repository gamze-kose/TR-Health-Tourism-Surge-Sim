## Gaps (figure not found on an opened page)

Length of stay and operations: no spinal cord injury rehabilitation stay, no amputee rehabilitation stay, no burn stay, no TBI stay. Number of reconstructive operations per patient is available only as a calculated all-specialty figure for MSF Amman. Limb-specific and burn-specific operation counts are missing. Rows in los_injury.csv placed under limb on case-mix grounds are labelled as such in the note.

recovery: no direct share of patients needing post-acute nursing or wound care after discharge. Only proxies are recorded (pressure ulcers, superficial wounds, discharge destination, beds occupied by pressure sores).

face_jaw and eye: no earthquake figure. Conflict figures are the Gaza medevac list and Kilis department shares only. Belmont 2012 does not separate face or eye.

Crisis types: nothing from Wenchuan 2008 or Kashmir 2005. Yemen appears only as a country of origin in the MSF Amman data. Afghanistan and Iraq appear only through US military wounds, with no civilian series. Ukraine has no quantified figure, because the only page opened gives none. Syria in Turkish hospitals is limited to one border hospital's department shares (no injury-level diagnoses).

wound_infection: no quantified figure for Gaza or Ukraine; no multidrug-resistance figure for Kahramanmaraş.

burns: not reported in the Kahramanmaraş series opened.

children: no Kahramanmaraş share of injured; the opened paediatric series is a clinic cohort.

amputation: prosthetic need is not quantified directly. Registered amputees at one Haitian prosthetics centre is the closest figure.

crush_aki: conflict settings not covered (no figure found).
